# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify all stakeholders involved in the SAP S/4HANA MM Greenfield Implementation project and define their responsibilities, involvement, and influence throughout the implementation lifecycle.

Effective stakeholder management ensures successful communication, timely decision-making, and smooth project execution.

---

# Stakeholder Overview

The project involves multiple business functions that participate throughout procurement, inventory management, approval workflows, invoice verification, and project governance.

- Project approval
- Budget approval
- Strategic decisions
- Project governance

- Timeline management
- Risk management
- Team coordination
- Progress reporting

# Success Factors

Successful stakeholder engagement requires:

- Clear communication.
- Active participation during workshops.
- Timely decision-making.
- User involvement during testing.
- Continuous project monitoring.
- Cross-functional collaboration.

# Conclusion

The success of the SAP S/4HANA MM Greenfield Implementation depends on effective collaboration among business stakeholders, project leadership, and the SAP MM implementation team.

Clearly defined roles and responsibilities ensure smooth project execution, reduce communication gaps, and support successful adoption of the new SAP procurement and inventory management processes.
