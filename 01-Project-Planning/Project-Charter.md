# Executive Summary

NovaTech Electronics Manufacturing India Pvt. Ltd. is a high-volume electronics manufacturing enterprise that procures production materials, consumables, imported components, IT assets, fixtures, and capital equipment required for uninterrupted manufacturing operations.

The current procurement landscape consists of multiple standalone legacy applications used independently for procurement, inventory management, approval workflows, and budget monitoring. As a result, procurement teams perform manual coordination between systems, increasing operational effort, reporting complexity, and the possibility of data inconsistencies.

To address these challenges, the organization has initiated a Greenfield implementation of SAP S/4HANA Materials Management (MM) to establish a centralized, integrated procurement and inventory management platform.

The implementation aims to standardize procurement processes, improve inventory visibility, streamline approval workflows, enhance vendor management, and provide real-time procurement reporting across the organization.

---

# Business Background

The organization manufactures consumer electronic devices through a high-volume production facility located in Sriperumbudur.

The manufacturing operation depends on the continuous availability of production materials, engineering fixtures, consumables, imported testing components, IT materials, and capital equipment.

The procurement department works closely with Planning, Inventory Management, Logistics, Finance, Cost Management, and Accounts to ensure timely procurement and uninterrupted production.

The procurement process begins with production demand planning and continues through Purchase Requisition, Purchase Order, Vendor Follow-up, Goods Receipt, Inventory Management, Invoice Verification, and Vendor Payment.

---

# Current Business Situation (AS-IS)

The existing procurement process operates using multiple independent business applications.

Current business functions are distributed across different systems for:

- Purchase Requisition
- Purchase Order
- Purchase Information Record
- Budget Monitoring
- Approval Workflow
- Goods Receipt
- Goods Issue
- Inventory Management

This fragmented environment creates several business challenges:

- Multiple applications are required to complete one procurement cycle.
- Procurement information is distributed across independent systems.
- Manual verification increases operational effort.
- Procurement reporting requires data consolidation from different platforms.
- Inventory visibility is not centralized.
- Approval tracking requires manual coordination.
- Vendor follow-up is time-consuming.
- Decision-making is delayed due to fragmented business information.

---

# Business Need

The organization requires a centralized ERP platform capable of integrating procurement, inventory management, material master data, vendor management, purchasing, and reporting into a single system.

SAP S/4HANA MM has been selected to eliminate process fragmentation, improve operational efficiency, and establish standardized procurement processes across the organization.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this implementation are:

- Implement SAP S/4HANA MM as a centralized procurement platform.
- Replace multiple legacy procurement systems.
- Standardize procurement processes.
- Improve inventory visibility.
- Digitize Purchase Requisition and Purchase Order processing.
- Implement structured approval workflows.
- Improve vendor management.
- Enable real-time procurement reporting.
- Reduce manual reconciliation activities.
- Improve procurement efficiency and business transparency.
