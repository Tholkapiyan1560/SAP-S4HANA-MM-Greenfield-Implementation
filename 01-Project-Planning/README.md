# Project Planning

## Overview

The Project Planning phase establishes the strategic foundation for the SAP S/4HANA Materials Management (MM) Greenfield Implementation project for a large-scale electronics manufacturing enterprise.

The organization currently operates its procurement, inventory management, approval workflow, and budget management processes across multiple independent legacy applications. Although each application supports a specific business function, the absence of system integration results in duplicate data maintenance, limited business visibility, manual coordination between departments, reporting inefficiencies, and delayed procurement decisions.

This project proposes the implementation of SAP S/4HANA MM as a centralized Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) solution to digitally transform the organization's procurement lifecycle by integrating purchasing, inventory management, vendor management, goods movements, invoice verification, and reporting into a single platform.

The implementation follows a Greenfield approach, where the SAP environment is designed and configured from scratch using industry best practices while aligning with the procurement operations of a high-volume electronics manufacturing environment.

---

# Business Background

The organization manufactures consumer electronic products through high-volume production facilities that require continuous availability of production materials, consumables, testing equipment, fixtures, IT assets, and imported components.

The procurement organization manages procurement activities for multiple business functions, including production operations, capital expenditure (CAPEX), engineering support, information technology, and manufacturing infrastructure.

The procurement process involves coordination among Planning, Procurement, Inventory Management, Logistics, Customs, Finance, Cost Management, and Accounts departments while working with more than 100 approved domestic and international suppliers.

Business continuity depends on timely procurement, accurate inventory visibility, vendor performance monitoring, and efficient approval workflows.

---

# Current Business Challenges

The existing procurement environment relies on multiple disconnected legacy applications for managing:

- Purchase Requisitions (PR)
- Purchase Orders (PO)
- Purchase Information Records
- Inventory Transactions
- Goods Receipt (GR)
- Goods Issue (GI)
- Budget Monitoring
- Approval Workflows

Because these business functions operate on different platforms, several operational challenges exist:

- Multiple software applications must be accessed to complete a single procurement cycle.
- Procurement data is distributed across different systems without real-time integration.
- Manual verification increases the possibility of reporting inconsistencies.
- Approval tracking requires coordination between multiple platforms.
- Inventory visibility is fragmented.
- Procurement reporting requires manual data consolidation.
- Vendor follow-up and material tracking consume significant operational effort.
- Decision-making is delayed due to limited centralized reporting.

These limitations reduce operational efficiency and increase the administrative workload across procurement and planning teams.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this implementation are:

- Implement SAP S/4HANA MM as a centralized procurement platform.
- Integrate procurement and inventory processes into a single ERP system.
- Standardize procurement workflows across departments.
- Improve inventory visibility and stock accuracy.
- Digitize Purchase Requisition and Purchase Order processes.
- Streamline approval workflows using SAP Release Strategies.
- Improve vendor management and procurement monitoring.
- Enable real-time procurement reporting and analytics.
- Reduce manual data reconciliation across multiple systems.
- Support scalable procurement operations for high-volume manufacturing.
