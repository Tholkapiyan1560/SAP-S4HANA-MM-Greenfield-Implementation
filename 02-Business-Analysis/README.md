# Business Analysis

## Overview

The Business Analysis phase focuses on understanding the organization's existing procurement operations, identifying business challenges, and defining the requirements for implementing SAP S/4HANA Materials Management (MM).

The objective of this phase is to analyze the current procurement process, identify improvement opportunities, and establish a clear business foundation for the SAP implementation.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze the existing procurement and inventory processes.
- Identify current business challenges.
- Gather and document business requirements.
- Design the future procurement process using SAP S/4HANA MM.
- Prepare the project for the Solution Design phase.

---

## Deliverables

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Business-Requirement-Document.md** | Defines the business requirements, objectives, current challenges, and expected outcomes of the SAP MM implementation. |
| **Process-Analysis.md** | Documents the current (AS-IS) process, proposed (TO-BE) process, process improvements, and overall procurement workflow. |

---

## Business Scope

The analysis covers the following procurement areas:

- Production Materials
- Engineering Fixtures
- Manufacturing Consumables
- Imported Testing Components
- IT Materials
- CAPEX Procurement
- Service Procurement

---

## Departments Involved

- Operations Planning (OPM)
- IE Planning
- Procurement
- Inventory Management
- Logistics
- Cost Management
- Finance
- Accounts

---

## Expected Outcome

Upon completion of this phase, all business requirements, procurement processes, and operational improvements will be documented, providing a solid foundation for the SAP S/4HANA MM Solution Design and Configuration phases.

---

## Next Phase

**Phase 3 – Solution Design**

The approved business requirements will be converted into an SAP solution by designing the enterprise structure, organizational structure, master data, procurement process, and SAP configuration blueprint.
