# Business Scenario

## Overview

The organization is a high-volume electronics manufacturing company where continuous material availability is required to support production operations.

The existing procurement process uses multiple independent systems for purchase requests, purchasing activities, inventory tracking, and approvals.

SAP S/4HANA MM is implemented to integrate procurement and inventory processes into a single centralized system.

---

# Existing Business Challenges

The organization faced the following challenges:

- Multiple systems for procurement activities
- Limited visibility of purchase status
- Manual coordination between departments
- Delayed approval process
- Difficulty tracking inventory availability
- Lack of centralized vendor information

---

# SAP Solution Approach

SAP S/4HANA MM provides an integrated procurement solution covering:

- Material requirement management
- Vendor management
- Purchase requisition processing
- Purchase order management
- Goods receipt
- Invoice verification
- Inventory visibility

# Implemented Scenario

## Consumable Procurement

Material:

SMT Wipe Roll

Purpose:

SMT Wipe Roll is required for daily production activities. Maintaining stock availability prevents production delays.

Process:

1. Requirement identified by production team
2. Purchase requisition created
3. Vendor selected
4. Purchase order issued
5. Material received through MIGO
6. Invoice verified through MIRO
