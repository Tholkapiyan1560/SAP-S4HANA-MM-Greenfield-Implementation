# Inventory and Financial Impact

## Overview

The SAP S/4HANA MM implementation improves inventory management by providing real-time stock visibility and integrates procurement activities with financial processes.

The system ensures accurate inventory updates, better material control, and proper financial tracking.

---

# Inventory Impact

## Improved Stock Visibility

SAP provides real-time information about:

- Available stock
- Material movements
- Goods receipt status
- Consumption details

This helps business teams make better inventory decisions.

---

## Stock Management Process

The inventory process includes:

- Purchase Order creation
- Goods Receipt posting
- Stock quantity update
- Material document generation
- Inventory monitoring

---

# Inventory Benefits

The implementation provides:

- Better stock accuracy

# Financial Impact

SAP MM integrates procurement activities with SAP Finance for accurate financial recording.

Financial impact occurs during:

- Purchase order creation
- Goods receipt posting
- Invoice verification

---

# Accounting Integration

## Goods Receipt Impact

When goods receipt is posted:

- Inventory value is updated
- Material document is generated
- Accounting document is created

---

## Invoice Verification Impact

During invoice verification:

- Vendor liability is created
- Invoice amount is recorded
- Tax details are updated
