# Phase 05 – Business Scenarios

## Overview

This phase documents the business processes, procurement strategies, and operational rules implemented during the SAP S/4HANA MM Greenfield Implementation project.

The objective is to demonstrate how business requirements are translated into standardized SAP procurement processes, ensuring efficient material planning, inventory management, and financial integration across the organization.

---

## Business Scope

The implementation covers three procurement categories:

- **Consumables Procurement** – Standard operational materials required for daily manufacturing activities.
- **Fixture Procurement** – Production fixtures procured through a strategic sourcing process involving RFQ and vendor evaluation.
- **Equipment Procurement** – High-value capital equipment procured through a controlled approval and procurement process.

---

## Deliverables

- Business Scenario
- End-to-End Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Process
- Business Rules
- Approval Workflow
- Inventory & Financial Impact
- Business Benefits & KPIs
- Challenges & Solutions
- Lessons Learned

---

## Business Outcome

The defined business scenarios establish a standardized procurement framework that improves process efficiency, inventory visibility, procurement control, and financial accuracy while supporting the complete Procure-to-Pay lifecycle in SAP S/4HANA MM.
