# Phase 05 – Business Scenarios

## Overview

This phase documents the business processes implemented during the SAP S/4HANA Materials Management (MM) Greenfield Implementation project for **NovaTech Electronics Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd.**

The objective of this phase is to bridge the gap between technical SAP configuration and real-world business operations by demonstrating how procurement requirements are translated into standardized business processes within SAP S/4HANA.

The implementation focuses on the complete **Procure-to-Pay (P2P)** lifecycle and explains the business logic, procurement policies, approval mechanisms, inventory impact, and financial integration across different procurement scenarios.

---

## Business Objectives

- Standardize procurement activities across the organization.
- Improve procurement transparency and document traceability.
- Ensure compliance with organizational purchasing policies.
- Maintain accurate inventory records.
- Integrate procurement with Financial Accounting (FI).
- Demonstrate end-to-end business process execution in SAP S/4HANA MM.

---

## Procurement Scenarios Covered

The business scenarios documented in this phase include:

- Consumables Procurement
- Strategic Sourcing for Production Fixtures
- Equipment Procurement

Each scenario follows SAP best practices while maintaining a consistent Procure-to-Pay (P2P) workflow.
